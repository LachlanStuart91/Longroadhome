THE LONG ROAD HOME - SELF-HOSTED WEBFONTS

Three families are self-hosted from this directory. No font is loaded from a
third-party CDN at runtime, so the site makes no external font requests.

  Bebas Neue   400  display        bebas-neue-latin-400-normal.woff2
  Oswald       600  supporting     oswald-latin-600-normal.woff2
  Inter        400  body           inter-latin-400-normal.woff2
  Inter        600  body emphasis  inter-latin-600-normal.woff2

Latin subsets only. Each family is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
The full licence for each is retained beside this notice and must be kept with
any redistribution of the font files.

Files were taken from the Fontsource packages recorded in package.json
(@fontsource/bebas-neue, @fontsource/oswald, @fontsource/inter), which package
the upstream Google Fonts releases unmodified.
